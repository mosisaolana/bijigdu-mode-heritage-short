# Design Thinking, Audience & Intent

## 1. Audience & Intent
- **Target Audience**: Family members, diaspora relatives, and viewers interested in the history of Emebet Bijigdu Mode, Guduru Malole Warada, and Wellega Awraja.
- **Viewing Context**: Mobile vertical (`9:16`, `1080x1920`) and widescreen/desktop (`16:9`, `1920x1080`), narrated in Amharic with burned-in English subtitles (`FontSize=8`).
- **Intended Feeling / Takeaway**: Viewers should feel pride and connection to a dignified, generous historical leader—remembered for her *Kaba*, *Zinar*, pistol, silver-chained mule, and service to her community.
